# The End of the Cash Era?

The shift toward Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDC) is not just a change in the form of money, but a complete redesign of the global monetary system. Unlike crypto, CBDCs are legal tender fully backed by the state.

### First: Anatomy of Digital Riyals and E-Dirhams
It's not just a database; CBDCs usually rely on Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) or blockchain to allow real-time verification of transactions without the need for multiple financial intermediaries, meaning transfers will move in seconds instead of days.

### Second: Impact on Monetary Sovereignty
In light of the growth of "private" digital currencies, central banks fear losing control over the money supply. CBDCs are the first line of defense to regain financial sovereignty and ensure the central bank remains the only trusted source of cash.

### Privacy Concerns:
The debate over CBDC raises serious concerns about "financial surveillance." Every penny spent will be recorded at the central bank, raising questions about the limits of state intervention in individuals' financial affairs.

# A Critical View by Ahmed Omar

As an accountant who analyzes flows, I believe CBDCs will be the **greatest revolution in capital tracking**. "Black holes" in the economy will disappear, and monetary policy will become more precise and responsive to crises. However, we must not sacrifice individual privacy at the altar of technical efficiency.
