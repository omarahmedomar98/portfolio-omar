# The Foundation of Financial Freedom

Financial success isn't about how much you earn; it's about how much you keep. Most people live paycheck to paycheck not because of their income, but because of a lack of a clear financial system. Managing your money is a skill that anyone can learn, and it starts with a few simple rules.

### The Three Pillars of Wealth:
1. **Budgeting (The 50/30/20 Rule):** 50% for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings/investments.
2. **The Emergency Fund:** Before you invest a single dollar, build a safety net of 3-6 months of expenses.
3. **Automate Everything:** If you wait to save "what's left" at the end of the month, you never will. Automate your savings the day you get paid.
